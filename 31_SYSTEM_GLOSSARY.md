# CommandOS System Glossary
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Define common terminology used throughout the CommandOS platform.

---

## Core Terms

### Executive Briefing
An AI-generated summary of the user's day, highlighting priorities, risks, calendar events, intelligence, and recommended actions.

### Mission Card
The primary work objective presented on the Home dashboard.

### Intelligence Feed
A curated stream of relevant operational, aviation, weather, and industry information.

### Command Palette
A keyboard-driven interface for searching, navigating, and executing actions.

### Service Layer
Application layer responsible for business logic and communication with external systems.

### Integration
A connection between CommandOS and an external provider such as Google Calendar, Supabase, OpenAI, or DroneSense.

### Organization
The top-level tenant used for authorization and data isolation.

### Project
A collection of related tasks supporting a common objective.

### Task
An actionable work item with status, priority, ownership, and due dates.

### Flight
A recorded or scheduled unmanned aircraft operation.

### Mission
A planned operational objective that may contain one or more flights.

### Property
A physical location managed for inspection, intelligence, or operational purposes.

### COPE
Construction, Occupancy, Protection, and Exposure property data.

### Thermography
Infrared inspection data collected for preventive maintenance and risk identification.

### AI Insight
A recommendation generated by the Executive AI with supporting rationale and confidence.

### ADR
Architecture Decision Record documenting important technical decisions.
