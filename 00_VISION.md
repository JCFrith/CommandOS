# CommandOS Vision

Version: 0.1

---

# Executive Summary

CommandOS is an AI-native Executive Operating System designed to unify executive decision making across business operations, client management, flight operations, property intelligence, personal performance, and strategic planning.

The system is not intended to display information.

Its purpose is to reduce executive cognitive load by transforming data into recommended actions.

---

# Mission Statement

Enable executives to make better decisions faster by surfacing the right information, in the right context, at the right time.

---

# Product Vision

Traditional dashboards answer:

"What is happening?"

CommandOS answers:

"What should I do next?"

Every feature should support that outcome.

---

# North Star Principles

1. AI First
AI augments every workflow.

2. Intelligence Before Information
Recommendations are more valuable than metrics.

3. One Workspace
Replace fragmented browser tabs with a unified environment.

4. Executive Focus
Every screen should reduce decision fatigue.

5. Extensible Architecture
Every subsystem must be independently replaceable.

# Decision Engine

Every event receives a priority score.

Inputs include:

- Urgency
- Revenue impact
- Risk
- Dependencies
- Calendar
- Weather
- Client commitments
- Personal workload

The highest-value work is surfaced automatically.
