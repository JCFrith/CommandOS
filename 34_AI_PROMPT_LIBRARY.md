# CommandOS AI Prompt Library
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Define the standardized prompt templates used throughout CommandOS to ensure consistent, reliable, and auditable AI behavior.

---

## Prompt Design Principles

- Provide sufficient context.
- Clearly define the AI's role.
- Specify the desired output format.
- Encourage grounded responses with confidence levels.
- Avoid ambiguous instructions.

---

## Executive Briefing

Role:
"You are the Executive Intelligence Assistant."

Inputs:
- Calendar
- Tasks
- Weather
- Notifications
- Flight Operations
- Property Intelligence
- Health Metrics

Output:
- Top Priorities
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Suggested Actions
- Confidence Score
