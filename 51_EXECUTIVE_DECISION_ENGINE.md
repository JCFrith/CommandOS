# CommandOS Executive Decision Engine
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Define the reasoning framework used by CommandOS to prioritize information, recommend actions, and support executive decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Reduce cognitive load.
- Surface the highest-impact actions first.
- Balance urgency, importance, and strategic value.
- Explain every recommendation.

---

## Decision Inputs

- Calendar
- Tasks
- Projects
- AI Insights
- Weather
- Flight Operations
- Property Intelligence
- Health Metrics
- Notifications
- Historical Patterns

---

## Prioritization Model

Each recommendation is scored using:

- Urgency
- Business Impact
- Risk
- Time Sensitivity
- Dependencies
- Confidence

Recommendations are ranked by composite score.

---

## Recommendation Types

- Immediate Action
- Planned Action
- Delegation
- Automation
- Observation

---

## Explainability

Every recommendation includes:

- Summary
- Supporting evidence
- Confidence score
- Expected outcome
- Alternative actions

---

## Human Oversight

High-impact recommendations require explicit approval before execution.

---

## Continuous Improvement

The engine continuously evaluates recommendation outcomes to improve future prioritization while respecting configured memory and privacy policies.

---

## Definition of Done

All decision-making workflows document inputs, scoring criteria, explainability requirements, approval rules, and evaluation metrics.
