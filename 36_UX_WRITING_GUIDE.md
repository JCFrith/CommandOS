# CommandOS UX Writing Guide
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Establish a consistent voice, tone, and language style across the CommandOS platform.

---

## Voice

CommandOS communicates like a trusted executive operations partner.

Characteristics:

- Clear
- Confident
- Professional
- Concise
- Helpful
- Calm under pressure

Avoid:

- Buzzwords
- Jargon without explanation
- Humor during operational workflows
- Alarmist language

---

## Tone

### Normal Operation

- Informative
- Efficient
- Positive

Example:
"Your morning briefing is ready."

---

### Warning

State the issue and the recommended action.

Example:
"High winds are forecast during your scheduled flight window. Consider delaying departure."

---

### Critical

State facts first.

Example:
"Mission cannot begin because required preflight checks are incomplete."

---

## Button Labels

Prefer verbs.

Good:
- Create Mission
- Review Report
- Start Flight
- Complete Task

Avoid:
- Submit
- Continue
- Click Here

---

## Error Messages

Structure:

1. What happened
2. Why it happened (if known)
3. What the user can do next

Example:

"Unable to connect to Google Calendar. Please verify your account connection and try again."

---

## Empty States

Every empty state should:

- Explain why nothing is shown
- Describe the benefit
- Offer a next action

---

## AI Responses

AI should:

- Cite confidence when appropriate
- Separate facts from recommendations
- Explain reasoning briefly
- Ask for clarification when confidence is low

---

## Accessibility

- Plain language
- Descriptive labels
- Avoid color-only communication
- Support screen readers

---

## Definition of Done

All new user-facing copy follows this guide and is reviewed for clarity, consistency, and accessibility.
