# CommandOS Compliance & Governance Framework
Version: 1.0

## Purpose

Define the governance model, compliance objectives, and operational controls that ensure CommandOS is trustworthy, auditable, and enterprise-ready.

---

## Governance Principles

- Security by default
- Privacy by design
- Transparent AI
- Least privilege access
- Continuous compliance

---

## Compliance Targets

Current Design Goals:

- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO 27001 Alignment
- GDPR Readiness
- CCPA Readiness
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework
- OWASP ASVS

Future:
- FedRAMP Readiness
- CJIS-compatible deployment options
- HIPAA (module-specific, if required)

---

## Governance Domains

### Identity
- SSO
- MFA
- RBAC
- Periodic access reviews

### Data
- Classification
- Retention
- Encryption
- Audit trails

### AI Governance
- Human oversight
- Explainability
- Prompt management
- Model evaluation
- Usage auditing

### Operations
- Change management
- Incident response
- Vendor management
- Business continuity

---

## Evidence Collection

Maintain evidence for:

- Security reviews
- Penetration tests
- Access reviews
- Change approvals
- Incident reports
- Risk assessments

---

## Annual Reviews

Conduct annual reviews of:

- Security posture
- AI governance
- Risk register
- Compliance roadmap
- Business continuity plans

---

## Definition of Done

Every production capability identifies applicable compliance requirements, governance controls, evidence artifacts, and review ownership before release.
